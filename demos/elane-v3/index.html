05 · Your visit

Nothing starts with “Same as last time?”

Every visit has a beginning, a middle and a finish. That structure is part of what makes the experience feel considered.

01

We listen first.

Ten quiet minutes for your goals, sensitivities, habits and what you absolutely do not want.

02

We tailor the ritual.

Products, pressure, tone, finish and pace are adapted to you—not whichever protocol happens to be next.

03

We protect the pause.

Appointments include breathing room. No stylist juggling three chairs. No therapist watching the clock every minute.

04

You leave with a plan.

Simple aftercare, what to avoid, what to repeat and when—or if—you actually need to come back.
