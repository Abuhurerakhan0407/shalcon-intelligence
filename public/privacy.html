Privacy Notice

Last updated: 29 August 2026

Draft for launch review. This notice describes the current Shalcon website lead-capture flow. It should be reviewed against the final legal entity, vendors, retention periods and production infrastructure before production launch.

What we collect

When you request an automation audit, Shalcon may collect the information you choose to provide, such as your name, WhatsApp number, business or company name, selected industry, calculator assumptions, selected service range, page/referral information and the time of submission.

Why we use it

We use submitted information to respond to your request, schedule or conduct an automation audit, prepare relevant recommendations, operate and improve the website conversion flow, prevent abuse and maintain business records.

Contact permission

The audit form asks for your permission before Shalcon contacts you about the request. You may ask us to stop contacting you at any time.

Calculator data

The opportunity-at-risk estimator is a planning tool. Values entered into it are assumptions supplied or adjusted by you. The result is an estimate and is not a promise of lost revenue, recoverable revenue or future performance.

Service providers

Shalcon may use hosting, automation, communications, analytics, scheduling and database providers to operate the website and respond to requests. The production vendor list will be finalized before launch.

Retention

Lead information should be retained only for as long as reasonably needed for the inquiry, business records and applicable legal obligations. A final retention schedule will be documented before production launch.

Sensitive information

Do not submit patient medical information, insurance claim details, candidate confidential records, payment-card data, passwords or other sensitive client data through the public audit form.

Your requests

You may contact Shalcon to ask about, correct or request deletion of information submitted through this website, subject to applicable legal and record-keeping requirements.
