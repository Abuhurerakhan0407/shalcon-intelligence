# Shalcon Intelligence — UDYAM Registration Activity Guide

Status date: 31 Aug 2026

Purpose: help the proprietor complete the UDYAM/MSME business-activity section without guessing sensitive identity, tax or KYC fields.

This is an operational preparation guide, not legal or tax advice. The proprietor must enter/confirm Aadhaar, PAN, GST applicability, address, bank and other identity/tax information directly on the official portal. Do not paste those values into project chats or repository files.

## Official portal

Use only the Government of India UDYAM portal:

`https://udyamregistration.gov.in/`

The official portal states that UDYAM registration is free, online, paperless and based on self-declaration. It also states that one UDYAM registration may include any number of manufacturing or service activities.

## Shalcon activity type

For the current Shalcon Intelligence business model, the operating activity is **Services**.

Current business model being classified:
- custom AI automation/software implementation for clients;
- planning and integration of software systems and business workflows;
- ongoing software/system support and managed optimization;
- no manufacturing activity is currently part of the approved Shalcon model.

## NIC choices matched to actual work

Use codes that describe work Shalcon genuinely performs. Do not add codes merely to make the registration look broader.

### Recommended primary activity

**NIC 62011 — Writing, modifying, testing of computer program to meet the needs of a particular client excluding web-page designing**

Why it fits: Shalcon's flagship work is custom client-specific automation/software implementation, including workflow logic, integrations and supporting application code.

### Recommended additional activity

**NIC 62020 — Computer consultancy and computer facilities management activities**

Why it fits: Shalcon plans and designs connected systems integrating software, business workflows and communication technologies, then supports client implementation.

### Recommended additional activity when managed support is offered

**NIC 62013 — Providing software support and maintenance to the clients**

Why it fits: the approved Healthcare offer includes managed optimization/support after implementation.

### Optional only when actually sold under Shalcon

**NIC 62012 — Web-page designing**

Add only if Shalcon Intelligence itself contracts for website/web-page design work as a business activity. It is not necessary merely because the agency's automation projects have web interfaces.

**NIC 62099 — Other information technology and computer service activities n.e.c.**

Use only for genuine IT/computer-service work that does not reasonably fit the more specific codes above. Do not use it as a substitute for a more accurate primary activity.

## Practical selection order

For the current AI Operations Systems Partner model:

1. Select **Services**.
2. Add **62011** as the strongest primary match for custom client automation/software builds.
3. Add **62020** for systems/technology consultancy and integrated-system planning.
4. Add **62013** for ongoing software support/maintenance.
5. Add 62012 or 62099 only if the proprietor confirms those activities are genuinely part of Shalcon's contracted services.

## Identity/tax field rules

- Business structure remains **sole proprietorship** unless the proprietor explicitly changes it.
- Trading name remains **Shalcon Intelligence**.
- Do not invent a GSTIN. Follow the portal's GST/PAN applicability logic and current law/accounting advice.
- Do not copy Aadhaar, PAN, OTP, bank numbers or raw KYC files into GitHub, chats, screenshots or project docs.
- If the portal asks for a fact not already owner-approved, the proprietor should verify it before submission rather than guessing.

## After registration

Record only non-secret operational evidence in the project:
- UDYAM registration completed: yes/no;
- registration date;
- UDYAM Registration Number only if the proprietor explicitly approves storing it in project records;
- selected service NIC codes;
- any action required for Razorpay/accounting readiness.

Do not commit identity documents, OTPs, bank details or raw KYC material.
