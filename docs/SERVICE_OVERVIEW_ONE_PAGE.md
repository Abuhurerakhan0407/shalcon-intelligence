# Shalcon Intelligence — Service Overview

## AI Operations Systems Partner
Shalcon designs and deploys connected AI workflows that move an inquiry or operational request through **intake → qualification → routing/booking → follow-up → system update → human escalation**.

The goal is not another chatbot. The goal is a repeatable workflow that is easier to operate, measure and improve.

## What Shalcon builds
### AI Front Desk
Structured web, messaging or voice intake that collects approved information, answers approved questions and sends requests to the correct next step.

### WhatsApp workflows
Permission-aware intake, reminders, follow-up and escalation using approved business messaging infrastructure.

### Voice workflows
AI-assisted calling where the use case, consent requirements and escalation rules make automation appropriate.

### CRM / database orchestration
Write request source, status, ownership and outcome into the operational system so teams do not depend on disconnected inboxes.

### Document workflows
Collect required files, track missing items and route them for authorized review.

### Operational reporting
Measure inquiries, completions, handoffs, errors and workflow outcomes before making ROI claims.

## Launch focus
**Primary:** Healthcare / clinics  
**Secondary:** EdTech / coaching / admissions  
**Adjacent supported workflows:** Insurance, E-commerce, HR / Recruitment

## Flagship system
### AI Front Desk + Lead Operations System
A scoped implementation can include:
1. inbound inquiry capture;
2. approved qualification/intake questions;
3. service/branch/intent routing;
4. appointment/demo scheduling;
5. no-response follow-up/reminders;
6. CRM/database write-back;
7. owner/team alerts;
8. human handoff for sensitive/low-confidence cases;
9. operational events for measurement.

Final scope depends on the client’s verified workflow, access and dependencies.

## Buying path
### Workflow audit
Map one important workflow, identify friction and define an appropriate automation starting point.

### Bounded pilot
Implement one production workflow with clear integrations, human fallback, acceptance tests and measurement.

### Connected system
Expand only after the pilot proves the workflow, adding channels/integrations/reporting/support through written scope changes.

## Boundaries
- No replacement of qualified human judgment for medical, legal, policy, hiring or other sensitive decisions requiring authorized review.
- No revenue, conversion, savings or platform-uptime guarantee unless a specific contractual guarantee is separately reviewed and approved.
- Public demonstrations use synthetic data unless explicitly identified as verified client deployment evidence.
- Client-specific data/security/retention requirements are resolved per engagement where applicable.
