# Shalcon Intelligence — Service Overview

## AI Operations Systems Partner
Shalcon designs and deploys connected AI workflows that move an inquiry or operational request from **intake → qualification → routing/booking → follow-up → system update → human escalation**.

The goal is not to add another chatbot. The goal is to make a repeatable business workflow easier to operate, measure and improve.

## What Shalcon builds

### AI Front Desk
Structured web / messaging / voice intake that collects approved information, answers approved questions and sends the request to the right next step.

### WhatsApp workflows
Permission-aware intake, reminders, follow-up and escalation using approved business messaging infrastructure.

### Voice workflows
AI-assisted inbound/outbound calling where the use case, consent requirements and escalation rules make automation appropriate.

### CRM / database orchestration
Write lead/request source, status, ownership and outcome into the operational system so teams are not forced to manage disconnected inboxes.

### Document workflows
Collect required files, track missing items and route them for authorized review.

### Operational reporting
Measure inquiries, completions, handoffs, errors and workflow outcomes before making ROI claims.

## Launch focus
**Primary:** Healthcare / clinics

**Secondary:** EdTech / coaching / admissions

Additional supported workflows: Insurance, E-commerce, HR / Recruitment.

## Flagship system
### AI Front Desk + Lead Operations System
A typical implementation can include:
1. inbound inquiry capture;
2. approved qualification/intake questions;
3. service / branch / intent routing;
4. appointment or demo scheduling;
5. no-response follow-up and reminders;
6. CRM/database write-back;
7. owner/team alerts;
8. human handoff for sensitive or low-confidence cases;
9. operational events for measurement.

Final scope depends on the client's real workflow and integration access.

## Buying paths

### Free automation audit
Map one important workflow, identify friction and define the safest/highest-value automation starting point.

### Pilot
One bounded production workflow with clear integrations, human fallback, acceptance tests and measurement.

### System
Broader multi-step/multi-channel automation with CRM orchestration, reporting, monitoring and ongoing optimization.

## Boundaries
Shalcon does not market automation as a replacement for qualified human judgment where medical, legal, policy, hiring or other sensitive decisions require authorized review.

Shalcon does not guarantee revenue, conversion, savings or platform uptime unless a specific written contractual guarantee is separately approved.

Public demonstrations use synthetic data unless explicitly identified as a verified client deployment.
