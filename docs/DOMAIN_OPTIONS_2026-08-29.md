## Recommendation

Use `shalconintelligence.com` as the production canonical domain unless the owner already controls a better exact-brand domain.

Why:
- exact brand match;
- familiar `.com` trust signal for B2B prospects;
- materially lower cost than `.ai`;
- avoids spending launch energy on domain cleverness rather than proof/outbound.

Do not add canonical/absolute Open Graph URLs to production source until the final domain is actually owned and connected.
