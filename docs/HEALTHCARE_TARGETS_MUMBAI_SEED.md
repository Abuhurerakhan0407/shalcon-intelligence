# Shalcon Intelligence — Mumbai Healthcare Prospect Seed List

Research date: 29 August 2026

Purpose: seed accounts for manual qualification. Public website observations are hypotheses for outreach, not claims about internal operations.

## 1. Meesha Health
Website: https://meeshahealth.in/
Type: Multi-specialty polyclinic / diagnostics
Public signal: Two Mumbai centres (Mahalaxmi and BKC), online appointment feature and booking portal.
Why interesting: multi-specialty routing + multiple locations creates a plausible use case for structured intake and branch/doctor routing.
Outreach angle: ask how web inquiries are routed across specialties/locations and whether follow-up status is centralized.
Priority: A

## 2. Divine Smiles Dental Care
Website: https://divinesmiles.in/
Type: Multi-location dental clinic
Public signal: Bandra West + Lower Parel branches, phone, WhatsApp and online booking options.
Why interesting: multiple booking channels and two locations make unified lead status/routing worth exploring.
Outreach angle: one intake layer that identifies branch + treatment intent before handoff/booking.
Priority: A

## 3. The Dental Hub Mumbai
Website: https://thedentalhubmumbai.com/
Type: Multi-location dental clinic
Public signal: Chembur East + Lokhandwala/Andheri West centres; appointments via call or WhatsApp.
Why interesting: two-location routing and appointment coordination are visible workflow opportunities.
Outreach angle: branch/treatment qualification and follow-up around WhatsApp inquiries.
Priority: A

## 4. VRX Healthcare Diagnostics
Website: https://vrxhealthcare.in/diagnostics
Type: Diagnostic network
Public signal: Three Mumbai centres; home collection; phone/WhatsApp/booking options.
Why interesting: location selection + test/service choice + home collection creates a multi-branch intake/routing workflow.
Outreach angle: structured test/home-collection intake followed by routing to the correct centre/team.
Priority: A

## 5. Agilus Diwine Diagnostics
Website: https://agilusbandra.com/
Type: Diagnostic centres
Public signal: Bandra West + Santacruz West centres, appointment booking and home-visit offering.
Why interesting: two locations plus home visits require service/location routing and scheduling coordination.
Outreach angle: capture test + location + home-visit intent before staff handoff.
Priority: A

## 6. Dr. Samira Siddiqui Dermatology
Website: https://www.samirasiddiqui.com/dermatologist-in-mumbai
Type: Dermatology clinics
Public signal: Three clinics; phone/WhatsApp appointment flow.
Why interesting: location choice and treatment/consultation intent can be structured before a human replies.
Outreach angle: location-aware WhatsApp intake and appointment routing with human escalation for medical questions.
Priority: A

## 7. Serenity Med Aesthetics
Website: https://serenityderma.com/
Type: Dermatology / aesthetics
Public signal: call, WhatsApp and online booking form; walk-ins also supported.
Why interesting: multiple inquiry channels around consultations and treatments.
Outreach angle: qualify consultation/treatment intent and preferred time before staff handoff; avoid any clinical advice automation.
Priority: B

## 8. Accura Diagnostics
Website: https://accuradiagno.in/book
Type: Diagnostics / pathology / imaging
Public signal: online test booking, slot selection, home collection and WhatsApp alternative.
Why interesting: structured booking already exists, so the stronger angle is cross-channel status, abandoned booking follow-up and staff notification rather than replacing the form.
Outreach angle: automate incomplete booking follow-up and unify WhatsApp/web inquiry status.
Priority: B

## 9. Urban Smiles Dental Care
Website: https://urbansmilesdentalcare.in/

Outreach angle: persist the booking intent before WhatsApp opens, then update outcome after confirmation.
Priority: B

## 10. Dental Studio 32

Type: Dental clinic
Public signal: appointment form + WhatsApp confirmation workflow.

Priority: B

## 11. Dental Den
