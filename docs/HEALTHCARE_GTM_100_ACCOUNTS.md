# Shalcon Intelligence — First 100 Healthcare Accounts

Goal: create a repeatable, compliant research and outreach process for the flagship AI Front Desk + Lead Operations offer.

## 1. Geography
Start with one tight market before expanding. Recommended launch wedge: Mumbai Metropolitan Region, then Pune / broader India after message-market fit is understood.

## 2. Account types
Prioritize:
1. Multi-doctor clinics
2. Diagnostic centers
3. Dental / dermatology / physiotherapy chains
4. Telemedicine operators
5. Specialty clinics with strong inbound lead flow

Avoid mass-scraping everyone. Research enough context to make the first message relevant.

## 3. Minimum account fields
- Business name
- Website
- City / area
- Clinic type
- Number of locations if visible
- Public contact channel(s)
- Decision-maker/contact when publicly available
- Observed inquiry path: form / WhatsApp / call / booking tool
- Existing scheduling/CRM clues
- One workflow observation
- Outreach status
- Next action date
- Opt-out / do-not-contact flag

## 4. Research signal checklist
Look for public signals such as:
- WhatsApp button but no structured intake;
- generic contact form with no booking flow;
- multiple doctors/services requiring routing;
- appointment requests handled manually;
- repeated FAQ volume visible from website/service mix;
- multiple branches;
- social ads / active lead generation;
- phone-only or WhatsApp-only booking;
- separate tools that appear disconnected.

Do not claim their internal process is broken merely from public evidence. Phrase observations as possibilities to validate.

## 5. Tiering
### Tier A — 25 accounts
High fit, meaningful volume signals, personalized research. Manual outreach only.

### Tier B — 50 accounts
Good fit, lighter research. Semi-templated but still personalized.

### Tier C — 25 accounts
Experimental segments used to test adjacent clinic categories.

## 6. First-message pattern
Observation → possible friction → concrete system → small CTA.

Example structure:
“Noticed [specific public observation]. For clinics with that setup, intake/booking often gets split across WhatsApp + staff follow-up. We build one workflow that qualifies, routes, books and updates the CRM while escalating anything sensitive to staff. Worth mapping against your current process for 15 minutes?”

Never say:
- “you are losing ₹X” without their data;
- “we increased clinics by X%” without verified evidence;
- “guaranteed appointments”;
- fake client counts or uptime;
- medical outcome claims.

## 7. Outreach cadence
Day 0 — personalized email or LinkedIn
Day 3 — one useful workflow observation
Day 7 — short follow-up
Day 14 — synthetic demo / architecture asset
Day 30 — close loop politely

Stop after opt-out or clear rejection.

## 8. Target throughput
Week 1: research 25 Tier-A accounts, contact 10–15.
Week 2: complete Tier-A outreach and begin Tier-B.
Week 3: assess replies, objections and audit-booking rate before scaling volume.
Week 4: continue only the segments/messages that produce qualified conversations.

The objective is not 100 messages quickly. It is learning which 20 accounts respond to which operational problem.

## 9. Weekly review
Track:
- accounts researched;
- contacts attempted;
- valid delivery rate;
- positive replies;
- audits booked;
- no-shows;
- objections;
- qualified opportunities;
- proposals;
- wins/losses.

Write down exact language prospects use to describe their problems. Feed that language back into website and sales copy.

## 10. Evidence loop
For every pilot:
1. Record baseline before deployment.
2. Define operational success metrics.
3. Collect post-launch measurements.
4. Ask client for permission before publishing.
5. Add only verified values to CLAIMS_REGISTER.md.

This turns outreach into the proof engine for Shalcon's future marketing.
