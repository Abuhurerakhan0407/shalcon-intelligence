## Personalization rules
Valid first lines reference something publicly observable and specific, such as:
- branch/location count;
- online appointment/contact path;
- multiple public booking channels;
- home collection / centre routing;
- multiple specialties/services;
- form → staff/WhatsApp handoff.

Do not personalize using unsupported assumptions such as:
- “your team is overwhelmed”;
- “you are losing patients”;
- “your response time is slow”;
- “you have X unanswered messages”;
- “we can recover ₹X for you.”

Those belong in discovery, if supportable at all.

## Healthcare boundary
Do not imply automation will:
- diagnose;
- recommend treatment;
- interpret reports;
- triage emergencies autonomously;
- make prescription/medication decisions;
- replace authorized clinical judgment.

## CTA hierarchy
Preferred: `Worth mapping for 15–20 minutes?`  
Alternative: `Want the short workflow?`

Avoid first-touch purchase pressure, guaranteed ROI, fake urgency or a request to send sensitive information.
