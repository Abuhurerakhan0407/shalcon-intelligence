## Personalization rules
A valid first line must refer to something publicly observable and specific, such as:
- branch/location count;
- WhatsApp + web form combination;
- online booking journey;
- home collection / appointment routing;
- multiple specialties/services;
- form → WhatsApp handoff.

Do not personalize using assumptions such as:
- “your team is overwhelmed”;
- “you are losing patients”;
- “your response time is slow”;
- “you have 200 unanswered messages.”

Those are discovery questions, not facts.

## CTA hierarchy
Preferred: “Worth mapping for 15–20 minutes?”
Alternative: “Want the 6-step workflow?”
Avoid: “Book a demo now”, “Buy”, “Guaranteed ROI”, or artificial urgency in first contact.
