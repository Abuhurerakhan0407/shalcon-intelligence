## 1. Market read

Published India/Mumbai pricing is fragmented because the market mixes simple SaaS-like WhatsApp bots with custom engineering agencies.

Observed current public examples include:
- simple WhatsApp/chatbot offers around ₹25k–₹60k setup with ₹5k–₹8k monthly management;
- lower-end voice/clinic automation products below that range where the buyer is effectively buying a standardized product;
- custom AI automation agencies publicly starting focused builds around ₹75k with retainers around ₹25k/month;
- broader dedicated/custom systems extending into multiple lakhs setup and ₹25k–₹60k+ monthly management.

Conclusion: Shalcon should not compete with ₹2k/month clinic SaaS. The flagship offer is a custom workflow/integration service with discovery, system design, safe escalation, write-back, UAT and measurement.

## 2. Approved launch offer — Healthcare Pilot

### AI Front Desk + Lead Operations Pilot
**Approved setup: ₹39,000**

**Approved managed optimization/support: ₹9,000/month** after the initial included stabilization period.

Third-party usage/platform costs stay in the client's own accounts or are separately itemized.

### Included scope
One bounded clinic lead/appointment workflow:
- one primary inbound path: web OR approved WhatsApp intake;
- approved FAQ/intake questions;
- qualification/routing rules;
- one booking/calendar or CRM/database write-back integration;
- one basic follow-up/reminder sequence;
- human escalation path;
- operational event logging/basic report;
- synthetic prototype;
- staging/UAT test cases;
- production handover;
- 14-day stabilization after acceptance.

### Explicitly not included at this price
- voice telephony agent;
- multiple branches with materially different workflows;
- multiple CRMs/ERPs;
- complex data migration;
- clinical diagnosis/treatment advice;
- custom mobile app/dashboard product;
- unlimited revisions;
- outbound campaign database building;
- third-party platform/API/message/call charges;
- regulated/compliance certifications or legal opinions.

Those trigger Growth/custom scope.

## 3. Why ₹39,000 / ₹9,000

This keeps Shalcon:
- above commodity bot/SaaS pricing;
- within a plausible founder-led entry range for a custom Mumbai clinic pilot;
- below the ₹75k+ starting point published by some custom agencies, reducing first-client friction while public proof is still being built;
- high enough to fund proper discovery, integration, UAT and stabilization rather than selling a loss-making “bot.”

Do not discount below **₹30,000 setup** for the defined pilot unless scope is reduced in writing or there is a deliberate strategic exchange approved by the owner.

Do not include open-ended monthly support below **₹7,500/month**. If the client does not want recurring support, define a paid change/support rate and clear post-stabilization boundary instead.

## 4. Growth system recommendation

**₹75,000–₹1,20,000 setup + ₹25,000–₹40,000/month** remains a sensible internal band when the project adds multiple workflow branches/channels, CRM orchestration, reporting and ongoing optimization.

Do not force a “Growth” package name in sales. Scope from the audit and present one recommended configuration.

## 5. Enterprise/custom recommendation

Keep **₹2,00,000+** as custom-scope territory where there are multiple departments/locations, advanced integrations, governance/security requirements, voice at scale or substantial operational complexity.

Never promise “unlimited capacity” unless third-party/system limits and economics support it.

## 6. Approved payment schedule

Implementation collection:
- **50%** at signature/start;
- **30%** when staging is ready for agreed UAT;
- **20%** on production acceptance.

Do not begin material third-party spend before the corresponding client funds are received unless the owner explicitly accepts the cash risk.

Monthly support is billed in advance.

## 7. Usage / vendor cost rule

Default: client owns and pays Meta/WhatsApp BSP, telephony, CRM, calendar, AI model, automation, hosting/database or other variable vendor usage wherever practical.

If Shalcon resells/bundles a cost, the proposal must state:
- included allowance;
- overage method;
- vendor price-change treatment;
- whether taxes are included/excluded;
- suspension behavior if a client fails to fund required usage.

## 8. Commercial risk limits

Do not include by default:
- guaranteed revenue;
- guaranteed conversion rate;
- guaranteed appointment volume;
- guaranteed response from third-party platforms;
- unlimited revisions;
- unlimited channels/integrations;
- unlimited support;
- fixed resolution SLA unless operations can actually support it;
- refunds based solely on business outcome rather than agreed deliverable/acceptance terms.

Shalcon guarantees the written deliverables/acceptance criteria, not customer behavior.
