# Shalcon Intelligence — EdTech Secondary Offer

## Admissions Intake + Follow-up Operations System

A connected workflow for coaching institutes, schools, training businesses and EdTech operators that need to capture, qualify, route and follow up admissions inquiries without creating another disconnected chatbot.

## The workflow Shalcon targets
Admissions inquiries can arrive through:
- website forms;
- WhatsApp;
- phone/callback requests;
- social campaigns;
- demo-class forms;
- counselor-managed spreadsheets/CRMs.

The useful automation opportunity is usually the operating path after that first inquiry: collect the required information, route it to the right course/team, schedule the next step, follow up when appropriate and keep status visible.

## Typical flow
1. Inquiry arrives from an approved channel.
2. System collects approved admissions fields such as course interest and contact preference.
3. Request is routed by program, location or learner intent.
4. Approved FAQ/course information can be provided from maintained source material.
5. Demo/counseling request is scheduled or handed to admissions.
6. Approved follow-up runs for incomplete/no-response leads.
7. Status is written to the CRM/database where integration permits.
8. Policy, payment, special-case or low-confidence questions escalate to staff.
9. Operational events are logged for measurement.

## Pilot deliverables
A bounded pilot may include:
- workflow map;
- intake/qualification fields;
- one primary channel;
- course/location routing;
- demo/counseling booking;
- follow-up sequence;
- CRM/database update;
- human escalation;
- synthetic/test-data prototype;
- UAT plan;
- event logging and stabilization.

Exact deliverables belong in the SOW.

## What stays human-owned
Examples:
- admissions exceptions;
- scholarship/discount approvals;
- refund disputes;
- policy interpretation outside approved content;
- high-stakes learner decisions requiring staff judgment;
- anything the system cannot verify from approved sources.

## What we measure
Start with operational indicators:
- inquiry response latency;
- intake completion;
- demo/counseling booking completion;
- follow-up completion;
- counselor touches per inquiry;
- routing/escalation accuracy;
- integration errors.

Enrollment or revenue impact should be claimed only when baseline/post-launch data supports it.

## Indicative commercial path
### Free audit
Map one admissions workflow and determine whether automation is justified.

### Focused pilot
A genuinely bounded single-workflow implementation may fit the Starter/Pilot planning range of ₹25,000–₹40,000, with ongoing support and third-party usage specified separately.

Broader multi-channel/CRM systems move into Growth/Enterprise planning ranges after discovery.

## Good-fit signals
- consistent inbound lead volume;
- repeated course/demo questions;
- manual counselor assignment;
- several follow-up steps;
- form → WhatsApp/call handoffs;
- multiple programs/locations requiring routing;
- unclear lead status across tools.

## Not a good fit
- very low inquiry volume;
- unclear/repeatedly changing admissions process;
- expectations of guaranteed enrollment growth;
- inaccessible/unsupported required systems;
- desire to automate decisions that should stay with authorized staff.

## Next step
Audit one real admissions path from first inquiry to demo/counselor/enrollment handoff, define success criteria and validate integrations before proposing a larger system.
