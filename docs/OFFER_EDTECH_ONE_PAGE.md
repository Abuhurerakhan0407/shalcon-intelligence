# Shalcon Intelligence — EdTech Secondary Offer

## Admissions Intake + Follow-up Operations System
A connected workflow for coaching institutes, schools, training businesses and EdTech operators that need to capture, qualify, route and follow up admissions inquiries without creating another disconnected chatbot.

## Workflow Shalcon targets
Admissions inquiries can arrive through website forms, WhatsApp, phone/callback requests, social campaigns, demo-class forms and counselor-managed spreadsheets/CRMs.

The useful automation opportunity is usually the operating path after first inquiry: collect approved information, route to the right course/team, schedule the next step, follow up when appropriate and keep status visible.

## Typical flow
1. Inquiry arrives from an approved channel.
2. System collects approved admissions fields such as course interest and contact preference.
3. Request is routed by program, location or learner intent.
4. Approved FAQ/course information is served from maintained source material.
5. Demo/counseling request is scheduled or handed to admissions.
6. Approved follow-up runs for eligible incomplete/no-response leads.
7. Status is written to CRM/database where integration permits.
8. Policy, payment, special-case or low-confidence questions escalate to staff.
9. Operational events are logged for measurement.

## Bounded pilot deliverables
A scoped pilot may include:
- workflow map;
- intake/qualification fields;
- one primary inquiry workflow;
- course/location routing;
- demo/counseling booking;
- follow-up sequence;
- CRM/database update;
- human escalation;
- synthetic/test-data prototype;
- UAT plan;
- event logging and stabilization.

Exact deliverables belong in the SOW.

## Human-owned decisions
Examples:
- admissions exceptions;
- scholarship/discount approvals;
- refund disputes;
- policy interpretation outside approved content;
- learner decisions requiring staff judgment;
- anything the automation cannot verify from approved sources.

## Measurement
Start with operational indicators:
- inquiry response latency;
- intake completion;
- demo/counseling booking completion;
- follow-up completion;
- counselor touches per inquiry;
- routing/escalation accuracy;
- integration errors.

Enrollment/revenue impact is claimed only when valid baseline/post-launch data supports it.

## Good-fit signals
- consistent inbound lead volume;
- repeated course/demo questions;
- manual counselor assignment;
- several follow-up steps;
- form → WhatsApp/call handoffs;
- multiple programs/locations requiring routing;
- unclear lead status across tools.

## Not a good fit
- very low inquiry volume;
- unclear/repeatedly changing admissions process;
- expectations of guaranteed enrollment growth;
- inaccessible/unsupported required systems;
- desire to automate decisions that should stay with authorized staff.
