# Shalcon Intelligence — Current Pricing Benchmark

Research date: 29 Aug 2026
Purpose: internal commercial calibration, not endorsement of competitor quality or truth of their performance claims.

Public prices change. Recheck before major repricing.

## 1. Custom agency / engineering examples

### Visiosoft Solutions — Mumbai
Source: https://visiosoftsolutions.com/ai
Public guidance: focused AI agent / WhatsApp automation projects start around **₹75,000 one-time** with ongoing support/optimization from **₹25,000/month**.

Signal: custom agencies can position substantially above simple bot subscriptions when selling implementation and operating workflows.

### OperateAI — India
Source: https://operateai.in/pricing
Public pricing page (June 2026):
- audit/roadmap **₹15,000–₹25,000**;
- build **₹45,000–₹1.2L**;
- retainer **₹15,000–₹25,000/month** (headline range shown publicly).

Signal: audit → build → ongoing operations is already a recognizable buying structure.

### Orange Labz
Source: https://www.orangelabz.com/pricing
Public “Build & Manage” range: **₹3.5L–₹7.5L one-time + ₹25K–₹60K/month** for a dedicated broader system, with lower-cost managed alternatives also shown.

Signal: multi-channel/dedicated architecture belongs in a materially higher price category than a standardized bot.

## 3. Standardized clinic/WhatsApp software examples

Some clinic-focused products publicly advertise prices around **₹2,000–₹3,000/month** with low setup/onboarding fees.

Examples found during research include:
- `mywhatsappclinic.com`
- `connectai.care`

These should not be treated as direct pricing comparables for Shalcon's custom integration offer. They demonstrate the danger of selling “WhatsApp appointment bot” as the whole value proposition: a standardized SaaS product can undercut an agency by an order of magnitude.

## 4. Strategic pricing conclusion

Shalcon should price against the work it actually performs:
- workflow discovery;
- integration design;
- data/safety boundaries;
- custom routing/write-back;
- UAT;
- failure handling;
- measurement;
- stabilization and optimization.

It should **not** defend a custom fee by claiming the chatbot itself is expensive.

This supports the V1 recommended Healthcare Pilot in `COMMERCIAL_RECOMMENDATION_V1.md`:

**₹39,000 setup + ₹9,000/month managed support/optimization**, with third-party usage paid separately/client-owned where practical.

That sits above standardized clinic SaaS, inside the observed lower/mid custom implementation market, and below many custom-agency entry points while Shalcon builds permission-backed production proof.

## 5. Repricing triggers

Raise or restructure when evidence shows:
- multi-location complexity is common;
- voice becomes standard scope;
- integrations require substantially more implementation/UAT;
- support burden exceeds assumptions;
- Shalcon develops reusable vertical components that materially improve outcomes/speed;
- permission-backed case studies reduce buyer risk;
- qualified demand consistently accepts current pricing without friction.

Lower price only by reducing scope or through an explicit strategic decision. Do not quietly absorb more work to match a low-cost SaaS competitor.

## 6. Research integrity

Competitor websites may contain their own marketing claims/testimonials. Shalcon's internal benchmark uses published offer/pricing information only; it does not treat competitor performance claims as independently verified evidence.
