# Shalcon Intelligence — Healthcare Flagship Offer

## AI Front Desk + Lead Operations System for Clinics
A connected intake and follow-up workflow for clinics, diagnostic centres, specialty practices and multi-location healthcare operators.

The system handles **operational coordination**, not medical diagnosis or treatment advice.

## Workflow Shalcon targets
Clinic inquiries can arrive through phone, WhatsApp, website forms, online booking and social channels. Before an appointment/request is confirmed, staff may need to identify location, specialty/service, preferred timing, contact details and the next responsible person/system.

Shalcon maps the real path first, then automates only appropriate, testable steps.

## Typical pilot flow
1. Inquiry arrives from an approved channel.
2. Approved intake fields are collected.
3. Request is classified by service/location/intent.
4. Routine approved questions use clinic-approved information.
5. Booking/request is routed to the configured calendar, CRM or staff workflow.
6. Eligible no-response/incomplete requests enter approved follow-up.
7. Status/outcome is written back where integration permits.
8. Medical, sensitive, unusual or low-confidence requests escalate to clinic staff.
9. Operational events are logged for review.

## Human-owned decisions
Unless separately designed and professionally approved:
- diagnosis;
- treatment recommendations;
- emergency assessment;
- prescription/medication decisions;
- interpretation of medical reports;
- sensitive complaints requiring professional judgment;
- exceptions outside approved workflow rules.

## Standard bounded pilot
A standard Healthcare Pilot can include, subject to final SOW:
- current-state workflow map;
- approved intake/question map;
- one primary inquiry workflow;
- routing/booking integration;
- configured follow-up;
- human escalation path;
- basic CRM/database status update;
- operational event logging;
- synthetic/test-data prototype;
- UAT test plan;
- production deployment + stabilization when dependencies are ready.

Expanded channels, integrations, branches, data migration or support scope are priced separately.

## Measurement standard
Start with operational evidence:
- inquiry volume handled;
- first-response latency;
- intake completion;
- routing/booking completion;
- follow-up completion;
- staff touches per inquiry;
- escalation volume;
- failed handoffs/integrations.

Revenue/appointment impact is discussed only when the clinic has a valid baseline and matching source data. No synthetic or illustrative result is presented as client performance.

## Data handling
- Public demos use synthetic data.
- Public audit form is not for patient medical information.
- Production data fields are minimized to workflow need.
- Client system access uses least privilege.
- Sensitive logging is minimized.
- Engagement-specific processing/security/retention terms are defined where applicable.

## Good-fit signals
Worth exploring when:
- meaningful inquiry volume uses multiple/manual channels;
- the same intake questions repeat frequently;
- requests require branch/service/doctor routing;
- booking/follow-up status moves between several tools/people;
- managers need clearer operational visibility;
- staff need automation with reliable human handoff.

## Not a good fit
Do not force the project when:
- volume is too low to justify automation;
- autonomous medical judgment is expected without appropriate professional review;
- required system access cannot be lawfully/technically provided;
- success cannot be defined/tested;
- expectation is guaranteed revenue rather than measurable operational delivery.
