# Shalcon Intelligence — Healthcare Flagship Offer

## AI Front Desk + Lead Operations System for Clinics

A connected intake and follow-up workflow for clinics, diagnostic centres, specialty practices and multi-location healthcare operators.

The system is designed to handle **operational coordination**, not medical diagnosis or treatment advice.

## The workflow Shalcon targets
Clinic inquiries often arrive through a mix of:
- phone;
- WhatsApp;
- website forms;
- online booking;
- social channels.

Before an appointment is confirmed, staff may need to identify location, specialty/service, preferred time, contact details and the next responsible person/system.

Shalcon maps that exact path and automates only the parts that are appropriate and testable.

## Typical pilot flow
1. Inquiry arrives from an approved channel.
2. System collects approved intake fields.
3. Request is classified by service/location/intent.
4. Routine approved questions can be answered from clinic-approved information.
5. Booking/request is routed to the configured calendar, CRM or staff workflow.
6. No-response or incomplete requests can enter approved follow-up.
7. Status/outcome is written back where integration permits.
8. Medical, sensitive, unusual or low-confidence requests escalate to clinic staff.
9. Operational events are logged for review.

## What stays human-owned
Unless explicitly designed and legally/clinically approved otherwise:
- diagnosis;
- treatment recommendations;
- emergency assessment;
- prescription/medication decisions;
- interpretation of medical reports;
- sensitive complaints requiring professional judgment;
- exceptions outside approved workflow rules.

## Pilot deliverables
A scoped pilot may include:
- current-state workflow map;
- approved intake/question map;
- one primary inquiry channel;
- routing/booking integration;
- configured follow-up;
- human escalation path;
- basic CRM/database status update;
- operational event logging;
- synthetic/test-data prototype;
- UAT test plan;
- production deployment + stabilization when dependencies are ready.

Exact deliverables are confirmed in the SOW.

## What we measure first
Prefer operational evidence before revenue claims:
- inquiry volume handled;
- first-response latency;
- intake completion;
- routing/booking completion;
- follow-up completion;
- staff touches per inquiry;
- escalation volume;
- failed handoffs/integrations.

Revenue or appointment impact is discussed only when the clinic has a valid baseline and matching source data.

## Data handling
- Public demos use synthetic data.
- The public audit form is not for patient medical information.
- Production data fields are minimized to what the workflow genuinely needs.
- Client system access should use least privilege.
- Sensitive logging is minimized.
- Data-processing/security terms are defined where required by the actual implementation.

## Good-fit clinic signals
The offer is worth exploring when:
- meaningful inquiry volume arrives through multiple/manual channels;
- the same intake questions repeat frequently;
- requests need branch/service/doctor routing;
- booking/follow-up status moves between several tools or people;
- managers want clearer operational visibility;
- staff need automation with reliable human handoff rather than autonomous clinical judgment.

## Not a good fit
Do not force the project when:
- the workflow is too low-volume to justify automation;
- the clinic wants autonomous medical advice without appropriate professional review;
- required system access cannot be lawfully/technically provided;
- success cannot be defined or tested;
- the expectation is guaranteed revenue rather than a measurable operational implementation.
