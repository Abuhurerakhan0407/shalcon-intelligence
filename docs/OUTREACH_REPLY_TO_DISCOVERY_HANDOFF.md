## Rule zero
A reply is not automatically a qualified opportunity.

When a prospect replies:
1. update the live Sheet to `Replied` only after the real response exists;
2. capture the actual reply meaning in concise Notes;
3. classify the response below;
4. reply from the same approved business channel where practical;
5. do not invent pain, urgency, budget, authority or timeline;
6. move to `Discovery Booked` only after an actual booking/confirmed meeting exists.
